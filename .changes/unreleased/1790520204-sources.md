title: The sources behind the figures, in the app
---

**Settings → About and sources** lists the published definition behind the adherence figure and the register the medication suggestions come from — each with a line on what it is used for, the words the app took from it, and a link to the source. It works offline; the link is the only thing that leaves the app, and only when you tap it. [Learn more](feature:sources)
