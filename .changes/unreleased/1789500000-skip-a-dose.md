doc: today

A dose you decide not to take is no longer recorded as one you forgot: long-press its row on **Today** — or right-click it, which opens a **Skip** menu — and the day stops asking for it. The row stays on the checklist, dashed and one press from being put back, and leaves the arithmetic entirely: it is not counted as missed, not counted as taken, and never named in the missed list. A day you set aside whole simply owes nothing. [Learn more](feature:today)
