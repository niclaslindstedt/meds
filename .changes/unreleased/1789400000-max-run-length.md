title: A longest stretch for when-needed medications
doc: medications
---

A medication taken when needed at no set times can now note how long it is meant to be taken for as well as how much of it in a day — a number and a **days / weeks** dropdown, beside the daily maximum. Every offer to log a dose then says where you stand — _Day 4 of 7_ — and at the end of the stretch the chip becomes **Log one anyway**. The stretch is counted from your own log, so a day you skip ends it and nothing has to be started or stopped. [Learn more](feature:medications)
