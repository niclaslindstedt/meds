type: Changed
title: The phone app keeps your log on the device
doc: native-app
---

The iPhone and Android app offers no cloud copy: there is no Sync section and no passphrase to choose, and your medications and log stay on the device. They leave it only in a backup you export yourself through the share sheet. Settings → About and the About screen say so — nothing is sent when the app opens, and nothing goes to iCloud or any other cloud — and link to the full privacy policy. If the device refuses to save, the message points at its storage space. The website and the desktop app keep their optional encrypted cloud copy, unchanged.
