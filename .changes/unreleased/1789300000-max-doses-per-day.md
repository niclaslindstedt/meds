type: Added
title: A daily maximum for when-needed medications
doc: medications
---

A medication taken when needed at no set times can now carry the most doses you mean to take in a day. Note the number you were given and every offer to log one says where the day stands — _2 of 3 today_ — and at the maximum the chip becomes **Log one anyway**, so a further dose is deliberate rather than reflexive. It is counted, never enforced: a dose you actually took is always loggable, and no other figure in the app moves because of it. [Learn more](feature:medications)
