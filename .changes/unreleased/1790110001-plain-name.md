title: The app is called Meds here
---

The browser tab, the installed app and the Dropbox folder read **Meds**. The
App Store listing may carry a longer name — that name belongs to the listing,
not to the app you are running.

If you sync to Dropbox, the folder is now `Apps/meds`: rename the old one to
match, or reconnect and let the app write a fresh copy.
