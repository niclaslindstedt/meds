doc: medications

A medication can now be marked **as needed** rather than scheduled. One with no set times is logged when you take it and sticks to that day; one with set times is started when you need it and puts its times on Today every day until you say you are done with it. Either way, the days you did not need it never count as missed. [Learn more](feature:medications)
